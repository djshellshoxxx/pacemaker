From the kit to the whole rig

1. Listen

Put Pacemaker on the drum bus or overheads, point a side input at the kick mic, or use the standalone with four mics, e-drum MIDI or triggers. It fuses what it hears and tells you how sure it is.

2. Predict

It tracks tempo, beat phase and bar position, holds steady through fills and breakdowns, and shows a confidence meter so you can trust it on stage.

3. Publish

Ableton Link, MIDI clock with start and stop, OSC with timestamped beats, and an audio sync pulse. All at once, in any host or none.

A count-in of four stick hits sets bar one. A song map with reference tempo and meter stops half-time and double-time mix-ups before they start. A drift report after the show shows what the band really did.

What you get that you could not get before

  • Lights and clips launch on one, not just at the right speed.
  • Fills, breakdowns and 6/8 do not throw the tempo, and you can see why.
  • Link, MIDI clock and OSC at the same time while following the drummer.
  • Logic, MainStage, Cubase, Bitwig, Reaper, QLab and hardware grooveboxes, not only Live.
  • A box on the stage with no laptop (planned).
