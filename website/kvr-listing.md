## Full description

The band sets the tempo. The machine keeps up.

Pacemaker listens to a live drummer (kick, snare, hat and overhead mics, e-drum MIDI, triggers or tap) and predicts the band's tempo, beat phase and bar position. It publishes that to everything that needs to stay with the kit.

**What it does**

- Follows the drummer: tempo, beat, bar and downbeat, with a confidence meter you can read from the other side of the stage.
- Stays steady through fills, breakdowns and 6/8, with Hold and Chase states instead of wandering.
- Outputs at the same time: Ableton Link, MIDI clock (with start/stop, optional song position), OSC with timestamped beats, an audio sync pulse for hardware clock boxes.
- Count-in: four stick hits set bar one. Fire a MIDI note or OSC message on the downbeat to start a song.
- Song map: reference tempo, meter and pattern per song, selectable by MIDI program change or OSC.
- Calibration: loopback and tap-along wizards, per-mic distances.
- After the show: drift log, report, and a tempo map exported as a MIDI file.
- Operator controls: Follow, Tap, Downbeat now, Nudge, Half, Double, Relock. All MIDI-mappable.

**Works with**

Ableton Live and Logic Pro through Link; Bitwig, Reaper (bundled scripts), Cubase and Studio One as a clock source for hardware; MainStage on MIDI clock; Pro Tools, Reason on Link (Pro Tools via the standalone until AAX); QLab, Resolume, VDMX, TouchDesigner and lighting consoles over OSC.

**Licence**

Perpetual licence for up to three of your machines, offline key file. 14-day trial: tracking always works, outputs pause briefly at intervals. Pacemaker Lite: Link output and one input role.

**Requirements**

A drum signal: at least one mic or trigger. A network connection is only needed for Link or OSC to other devices.
